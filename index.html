Frequently asked questions

What is Rateo?+

Rateo is a bill tracker for buy now pay later (BNPL) and installment plans. It brings plans from different providers into one dashboard and one monthly calendar, with reminders before each due date.

Is Rateo free?+

Yes. The free version includes up to 3 active plans. Rateo Pro removes the limit and adds the purchase simulator, spending analysis, wishlist, calendar sync with a Home widget, and backup/export.

When do reminders arrive?+

48 hours and 24 hours before each due date, as notifications on your phone. You can turn them off in Settings.

Does Rateo sync with my phone's calendar?+

Yes, with Rateo Pro. Turn on calendar sync in Settings and Rateo writes one event per installment into your phone's calendar, with the provider, the plan name and the due date (not the amount). If that calendar is linked to an account such as Google, the account syncs the events on its own servers. Turning sync off doesn't delete events already written: remove them from your Calendar app.

Do I need an account or to connect my bank?+

No. There is no account, no login and no bank connection. You enter your plans yourself, and the data of your plans stays on your phone.

Which providers can I track?+

You can choose from PayPal, Klarna, Afterpay, Amazon, Scalapay, Shop Pay, Clearpay, Affirm, Alma and Zip, and mark whether you pay by credit card or bank account.

Does Rateo lend money or pay my installments?+

No. Rateo is not a loan, mortgage or investment app: it doesn't provide credit and doesn't replace your BNPL provider or your bank. It is a tool to keep track of what you owe and when.

Is Rateo affiliated with Klarna, Afterpay, PayPal or the other providers?+

No. Provider names are used only to describe what you can track. Rateo is not affiliated with them.

Which platforms and languages are supported?+

iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). The app is available in English, Italian, German and Spanish.
