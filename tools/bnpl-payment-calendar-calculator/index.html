BNPL payment calendar calculator

Fill in a purchase the way you would in the Rateo app and see its due dates appear in the calendar, exactly as the app shows them. It runs in your browser: nothing is sent or saved.

App preview

This is a preview of the Rateo app: same form, same calendar. Here nothing is saved and nothing leaves your device; in the app your plans stay on your phone and you get a reminder 48 and 24 hours before each payment.

Try this in the Rateo app

Rateo does this for every plan you add: the calendar fills in by itself and you get a reminder 48 and 24 hours before each payment. No account, no bank access, and your data stays on your phone. Free up to 3 active plans.

Available for iPhone and Android

How it works

  1. Fill in the purchase: item, merchant, total, provider, number of installments and the date of the first payment.
  2. Tap “Save installment plan”: the plan appears in the calendar below. Add as many as you like.
  3. Check the calendar: days with a ring are due dates, and the list shows the payments due in the next 30 days.

What it covers

  • A preview of the Rateo app: installments are monthly, with the same amount each time (the total divided by the number of installments), as in the app.
  • Interest-bearing financing, fees and late charges are not calculated. Your plan summary or the provider’s app always prevails.
