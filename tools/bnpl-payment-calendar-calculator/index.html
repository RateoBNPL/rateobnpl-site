BNPL payment calendar calculator

Add your pay later purchases and see every due date in one calendar. Payments that fall close together are highlighted. It runs in your browser: nothing is sent or saved.

Try this in the Rateo app

Rateo does this for every plan you add: the calendar fills in by itself and you get a reminder 48 and 24 hours before each payment. No account, no bank access, and your data stays on your phone. Free up to 3 active plans.

Available for iPhone and Android

How it works

  1. Pick the provider and plan: the number of payments and the interval are filled in for you.
  2. Add every plan you have. The calendar merges them and sorts them by date.
  3. Look at the highlighted stretch: it is where the most money leaves your account in the shortest time.

What it covers

  • Schedules for Klarna (IT, DE, ES, US), PayPal (IT, ES, DE, US), Scalapay and Afterpay, following the official pages we checked on 23 November 2026. Start dates depend on the store and on your approval.
  • Interest-bearing financing, late fees and country-specific variants are not calculated.
  • Not legal or financial advice. Rateo is not affiliated with these providers.
