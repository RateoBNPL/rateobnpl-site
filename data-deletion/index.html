Data Deletion

Last updated: November 2026

Data on your device

Rateo - BNPL Tracker stores all your plan data (payment plans, amounts, providers, categories, preferences) exclusively on your device. This data is automatically and permanently deleted when you uninstall the app — no further action is needed.

Data outside your device

Some data is not stored on your device and is handled by our provider RevenueCat, Inc. on behalf of Rateo: your PRO purchase history, an anonymous identifier generated by the app, and some non-identifying technical information (app language, approximate range of the number of plans, the screen from which the PRO page was opened, how many times it was opened, the date of the last opening and the last action taken on it).

How to request deletion

Write to info@rateobnpl.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request” and include:

  • the store you used (Google Play or App Store);
  • the order number of your purchase: on Google Play, the code starting with GPA. in your receipt email; on the App Store, the order ID on your Apple receipt.

We will process your request within 30 days.

If you have never purchased PRO

The data linked to the anonymous identifier cannot be used to identify you. Uninstalling and reinstalling the app normally generates a new identifier.

Please note

Deleting this data does not cancel an active subscription. You can manage or cancel it from Google Play or the App Store.
