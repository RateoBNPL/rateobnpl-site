Klarna Pay in 4: the payment calendar, and how to avoid overlapping due dates

Short answer. Pay in 4 splits a purchase into four equal payments: one at the start, then one every two weeks, about six weeks in all. The date that matters is the first charge, which depends on the store. Write each plan's four dates in one calendar and check them before you start another.

How the calendar works

  • Four equal payments. On a $100 purchase that is four times $25.
  • The first payment is taken at checkout at some stores, and when the order is confirmed or shipped at others. Check the date in the plan summary or in the Klarna app.
  • The next three are charged automatically every two weeks to the card you chose.
  • Klarna sends a reminder before each payment, and you can update the card in the app.

How plans pile up

A simple example. Three $100 purchases, each split in four ($25 each), started three days apart (these are illustrative dates):

PlanPayment 1Payment 2Payment 3Payment 4
A (Tue 3 Nov)3 Nov17 Nov1 Dec15 Dec
B (Thu 5 Nov)5 Nov19 Nov3 Dec17 Dec
C (Sat 7 Nov)7 Nov21 Nov5 Dec19 Dec

Every fortnight, three payments fall within five days: $75 leaves your account in one window, even though each plan looks like only $25. Add a fourth plan from another provider and the window gets busier. Each app shows only its own.

How to avoid overlaps

  1. Put the four dates of every plan in one calendar the day you buy.
  2. Look at the next six weeks before you split another purchase. If a fortnight already has two or three payments, wait a few days or choose a different date for the new plan.
  3. Check the first-charge date when the store charges at shipping rather than at checkout: it moves the whole schedule.
  4. Keep the paying card funded on every date, especially around payday and rent.
  5. Mark each payment as paid so the calendar shows only what is left.

FAQ

How often does Klarna Pay in 4 charge?

Every two weeks, after the first payment. The whole plan lasts about six weeks.

When is the first payment?

At some stores at checkout, at others when the order is confirmed or shipped. Check the plan summary.

Can I change the card?

Yes, in the Klarna app.

Does Klarna remind me?

Yes, before each payment. A single list of your own is a second safety net if you have plans with several providers.

What if two payments land on the same day?

Both are charged on their own dates. Make sure the card has enough for the total.
