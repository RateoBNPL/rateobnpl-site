How to track Klarna, Afterpay and Affirm payments in one place

Short answer. Write down each purchase once, add every remaining installment with its date, amount and provider, set a reminder before each one, and check a single calendar every week. You don't need to link your bank account to do it.

Why the provider apps aren't enough

Each provider app shows only its own plans. Klarna's Pay in 4 and Afterpay both take the first payment at checkout and the rest every two weeks, so two plans started a few days apart can put payments in the same fortnight, and Affirm's due dates depend on the plan you chose. No single app shows the total. That is how a $30 payment from one app and another from a second app land in the same week as rent.

What to write down for every plan

  • What you bought and where
  • Provider (Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm…)
  • Total price and number of installments
  • Amount and due date of each installment
  • Which card or account is charged

Step by step

  1. Log the purchase on the day you make it. Do it before you close the checkout page, while the schedule is on screen.
  2. Add every installment, including the first. The first payment is usually taken at checkout, so it is already paid. Mark it as done and schedule the rest.
  3. Set a reminder before each due date. Provider reminders exist: Klarna sends them before each payment, Afterpay sends reminders and shows the schedule in its app, and Affirm sends an email or SMS before each payment and offers autopay, which you have to switch on. Your own reminder is a second safety net, in one place.
  4. Check the next 6 weeks before you split another purchase. Overlaps are visible in advance, so you can decide whether the new plan fits.
  5. Keep the paying card funded. A reminder doesn't help if the card is empty on the due date.
  6. Mark each payment as paid so the list always shows only what is still to come.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting that the first payment leaves your account immediately.
  • Starting a new plan without looking at the next six weeks.
  • Changing the card in one app and not remembering which plans use it.
  • Trusting reminders from each provider instead of one list.

FAQ

Do I need to connect my bank to track BNPL payments?

No. You can enter each plan by hand, which also means no one else gets access to your bank login.

Does Klarna Pay in 4 charge automatically?

Yes: the remaining payments are charged automatically to the chosen card, so the card needs to be funded on each due date.

What is the difference between Klarna's and Afterpay's schedule?

Both use four payments with the first at checkout. Check the terms of your own plan, because they vary by country and retailer.

How many plans can I handle at once?

Surveys show that most BNPL users have had more than one at the same time. The number matters less than whether every due date is in one list.
