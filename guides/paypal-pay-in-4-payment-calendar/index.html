PayPal Pay in 4: the payment calendar, and how to keep track of every due date

Short answer. Pay in 4 splits a purchase into four payments: one at checkout, then one every two weeks, a little over six weeks in all, charged automatically to the payment method you confirmed. Write the four dates in one calendar the day you buy, next to your plans from other providers.

How the schedule works

  • Four payments. The first (the down payment) is one quarter of the purchase and is taken at the time of the transaction. On a $100 purchase that is four times $25.
  • The next three repayments are taken automatically every two weeks.
  • PayPal shows the schedule while you apply, in the payment summary after approval, and in the Pay Later section of the PayPal app or website, where you can open the plan and see the payment schedule.
  • PayPal says it charges no sign-up, application or late fees on Pay in 4 (check the terms of your country and plan). Your bank may still charge a fee if a repayment is returned.
  • Availability, limits and rules depend on your country, the store and your own approval. In the US, PayPal shows Pay in 4 for carts between $10 and $2,000 when you check out with PayPal.

How a PayPal plan lines up with other plans

Illustrative dates. A $100 PayPal plan bought on Tuesday 3 November, and a $100 Klarna plan bought two days later:

PayPal (Tue 3 Nov)3 Nov17 Nov1 Dec15 Dec
Klarna (Thu 5 Nov)5 Nov19 Nov3 Dec17 Dec

Both plans charge every fortnight, so $50 leaves the account within the same two days, every time. Neither app shows the other's plan.

What you can do inside PayPal

  • Pay early or add a payment. From the Pay Later section, open the plan and choose Make a Payment. PayPal says there is no penalty fee for paying off the balance early.
  • Change the payment method for future repayments from the same section.
  • Know that approval is for one checkout. If you switch payment method during checkout and do not switch back to Pay in 4, the purchase is charged to the method you switched to.

How to keep track

  1. Enter the four dates when you buy, with the amount and "PayPal" as the provider.
  2. Mark the first payment as paid, since it was taken at checkout.
  3. Look at the next six weeks before you pay with Pay in 4 again. Two or three plans in the same fortnight add up.
  4. Keep the funding account or card covered on each date, especially around rent and payday.
  5. Set your own reminder a day or two before each repayment.

FAQ

How often does PayPal Pay in 4 charge?

The first payment at checkout, then every two weeks. The plan lasts a little over six weeks.

Where can I see my PayPal payment schedule?

During the application, in the payment summary, and in the Pay Later section of your PayPal account.

Can I pay a plan off early?

Yes. PayPal says there is no penalty fee for paying off the balance early.

Is Pay in 4 the same everywhere?

No. In Italy and Spain PayPal's no-interest option is Pay in 3 (three monthly payments), and Germany has different offers. Check the page for your country.

Does PayPal remind me?

Check your notifications and emails in your account. A single list of your own is a second safety net when you also use other providers.
