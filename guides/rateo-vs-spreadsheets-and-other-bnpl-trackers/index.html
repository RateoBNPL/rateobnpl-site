Rateo vs spreadsheets and other BNPL trackers: how to choose

Short answer. With one or two plans, a calendar event or a simple spreadsheet is enough. When plans from several providers start to overlap and you want reminders and a single total, a BNPL tracker app saves time. Choose by what you need: reminders, privacy, your phone and your language. This page compares the options, including where Rateo is not the best fit.

Four ways to track pay later payments

OptionGood atLimits
Provider apps (Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm…)Free, official dates and reminders for their own plansEach app shows only its own plans, so no total across providers
Spreadsheet or Notion templateFull control, free or cheap, works on any deviceYou enter and update dates by hand, no reminders unless you build them, easy to stop updating
Calendar with one event per paymentReminders on your phone, nothing to installEach event is created by hand, no total, no list of what is still to pay
BNPL tracker appDates calculated for you, reminders, all providers in one listVaries by app: some need an account, a bank or email connection, or a subscription

What to check in any tracker app

  • Does it run on your phone (iPhone, Android)?
  • Does it ask for an account, or for access to your bank or email?
  • Where is your data stored: on the phone or on a server?
  • Does it remind you before each due date, and how early?
  • Does it handle several providers, your currency and your language?
  • What is free, what is paid, and are there ads?

Where Rateo fits

  • Works on iPhone and Android, in English, Italian, German and Spanish.
  • No account and no bank login. The data of your plans stays on your phone, and there are no ads.
  • You enter the purchase, the provider and the number of installments; the calendar is built for you.
  • Reminders 48 and 24 hours before each payment.
  • PRO adds synchronization with the phone's calendar, a home screen widget, a purchase simulator, spending analysis, a wishlist and backup of your data.
  • Free up to 3 active plans. PRO is available as an annual plan or a lifetime purchase: check the store for current prices.

Where Rateo may not be the right fit

  • You type each plan by hand. There is no automatic import from your provider, bank or email. That is a deliberate choice, because it means no one needs access to your accounts, but it takes a few seconds per purchase. Some other apps do import data.
  • You have a single plan. A calendar event is enough.
  • You want a full budgeting app. Rateo is built around BNPL installments, not around your whole budget or debt payoff.

FAQ

Is a spreadsheet enough to track BNPL payments?

Yes, if you have few plans and you keep it updated. The weak points are reminders and remembering to update it after each purchase.

Do I need an app?

No. An app helps when plans overlap and you want reminders and a single list without doing the calendar yourself.

Does Rateo connect to my bank or to Klarna and Afterpay?

No. You enter each plan by hand. Rateo asks for no account and no bank login.

Is Rateo free?

The free version covers up to 3 active plans and reminders. PRO unlocks more features and is a paid in-app purchase.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. The only external service is the one that handles the PRO purchase.
