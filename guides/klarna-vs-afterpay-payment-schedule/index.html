Klarna Pay in 4 vs Afterpay: the payment schedules compared

Short answer. Both split a purchase into four payments, two weeks apart, over about six weeks. The difference that matters is the first payment: Afterpay takes it at purchase, while with Klarna it depends on the store. If you use both, a payment can leave your account every single week.

  • Both are free if you pay on time. Afterpay charges late fees if you don't; amounts and caps vary by country, so check yours.
  • With Klarna you can update the card in the app. Check the plan summary for the exact first-payment date.

How to keep both in one calendar

  1. Log each plan on the day you buy it, with the provider and all four dates.
  2. Check the first-charge date for Klarna. If the store charges at shipping, the whole schedule starts later than the purchase.
  3. Look at the next six weeks before you start another plan, and count payments from every provider together.
  4. Keep the paying card funded on each date.
  5. Mark each payment as paid, so the calendar shows only what is left.

FAQ

Is Klarna Pay in 4 the same as Afterpay?

The structure is similar: four payments two weeks apart. The first-payment date and the apps are different.

Which one charges first?

Afterpay at purchase. Klarna depends on the store: at checkout, or when the order is confirmed or shipped.

Do both remind me?

Yes: Klarna before each payment, Afterpay in its app. Neither shows the other's plans.

Can I use both at once?

Yes, they are independent services. You are the one who has to add up the total.
